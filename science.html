The science, with its limits

Read this page as “what Unicity's files present,” not as independent proof. Where a study is small, internal, or preclinical, this page says so.

GLP-1, as Unicity presents it

In plain words: GLP-1 is a signal your gut sends to your brain when you eat, telling you you are full. Unicity's explainer says it works in the brain (fullness and appetite), the pancreas (helps your body use sugar properly), and the stomach (food stays there longer, so you feel full longer). Natural compounds from yerba mate, as presented, are broken down by gut bacteria to raise your body's own GLP-1.

A four-week study summary compares a Unimate group with a water-only control group, with active GLP-1 significantly higher in the Unimate group, and body weight steady with Unimate while the control group gained. Unicity's own files give different percentages for the GLP-1 rise in different documents, so this site does not quote a single percentage for it — the direction is presented, the exact figure is not settled in the materials.

That four-week work is also labelled preclinical in Unicity's clinician files. Do not read it as a human weight-loss result.

Balance and blood sugar — the sensor work

In plain words: CGM means a small sensor worn on the body that checks blood sugar all day. The most specific human data in Unicity's set come from that kind of sensor study, tested on just 9 people:

  • Balance before a meal: post-meal glucose 34% lower at 30 minutes and 37% lower at 45 minutes, in that n=9 work.
  • A separate high-carbohydrate test meal: 43% lower post-meal glucose response and 37% lower peak glucose, as presented in the clinician leave-behind.
  • An earlier fibre study, cited to a 2011 University of Sydney study: post-meal glucose reduced by 28% and insulin response by 27%.

These are different endpoints and different studies, not one number repeated. All are as presented in Unicity's files, from small and largely internal work.

The 60-day Feel Great programme, as presented

Unicity's clinician materials present a 60-day internal programme in generally healthy adults, with these percent changes from day 0 to day 60:

  • Blood sugar swings up and down, down 12%
  • Total cholesterol down 5.7%, bad cholesterol (LDL) down 7.6%, another blood fat called triglycerides down 9.0%
  • HbA1c (a 3-month average blood sugar test) down 1.3%, waist down 2%, weight down 1%
  • Positive feelings up 18% in the medical professional guide's rounding

Appetite work in the same set: lower “desire to eat” as soon as 15 minutes after Unimate versus placebo, within-subject comparison n=9 over 120 minutes, as presented.

The patent — the fiber matrix

Balance is protected by US Patent #US20100074969A1, "Method of controlling blood sugar levels, insulin levels, cholesterol levels, body fat levels, and body weight by administering a nutrient fiber matrix" (filed March 2010, owner Unicity International, Inc.). As the patent describes it, the matrix works by: sequestering carbohydrates and slowing absorption, forming a thick gel with water that slows stomach emptying and keeps you full longer, pushing carbohydrates through faster so less is absorbed, and smoothing glucose spikes over time. The patent protocol is about 5–15 minutes before at least one meal, preferably before the highest-carbohydrate meals. Follow the pack label, not the patent, for how you actually use it.

The blends inside Balance

Biosphere Fiber Proprietary Formula — five fiber sources, presented for cholesterol support and slowing high-glycemic carbohydrate conversion. Unicity 7X — plant-derived viscous fibers that form the gel, slowing stomach emptying and prolonging fullness. Bios Cardio Matrix — plant extracts and phytosterols that slow cholesterol absorption. Bios Vitamin Complex — presented for healthy metabolism, digestion, and turning food into fuel.

How Balance works — three mechanisms

1. Fiber-mediated glucose regulation: fiber paired with carbohydrates slows digestion so glucose is better regulated, instead of spiking and crashing. 2. Satiety support: viscous soluble fibers form a gel in the digestive tract, slowing stomach emptying. 3. Cholesterol management: phytosterols in the Bios Cardio Matrix slow cholesterol absorption when taken with food.

The carb crash problem, in plain words

Without enough fiber, glucose enters the blood too fast — spike, then crash. That roller coaster is presented as the cause of fatigue and cravings over time. Modern ultra-processed food, the paper argues, gives more sugar and less fiber, making it hard to get enough fiber from diet alone. Balance's soluble fiber, taken with food, is presented as slowing that rise to a lower, slower curve.

What is NOT shown — read this before you buy on science alone

  • No large, long-term, peer-reviewed human trial of the full Feel Great programme with hard clinical endpoints (diabetes remission, cardiovascular events, sustained weight loss) appears in Unicity's library set reviewed here.
  • No head-to-head trial against a GLP-1 drug, against another fiber product at the same dose, or against time-restricted eating alone appears in the set.
  • Human data presented are short-term (notably 60 days), small-sample where n is given (n=9 for the CGM and appetite work), and substantially internal Unicity studies.
  • Social slides in the set use unqualified “no side effects” wording. Unicity's clinician files use narrower wording: generally well tolerated, with no serious adverse events reported in trials. This site uses the clinician wording.
