Balance — the fiber drink

Balance is the drink you take with your biggest meal. As presented in Unicity materials: a fiber matrix that forms a gel in the gut, slows the glucose rise after eating, and helps you feel full. It is a supplement, not a drug, and not a substitute for medical care.

As presented: about 4 g soluble fiber per serving, 0 g sugar (Balance box, from Unicity's own infographic). Balance Apple, the upgraded version, is presented with about 5 g fiber, a seven-fiber matrix, apple polyphenols and apple cider vinegar, and added psyllium — Natural Apple flavour only.

Honest notes: fiber can cause bloating or gas, especially at first. The files conflict on medication timing around Balance, so this site publishes no single hour-gap — follow the pack label and ask your provider or pharmacist. Metamucil's FDA ≥7 g/day psyllium claim does NOT transfer to Balance at 4 g — see Compare.
