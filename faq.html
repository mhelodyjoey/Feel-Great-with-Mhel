FAQ, answered honestly

If a question has a conflicting answer in Unicity's own materials, this page says so instead of picking one quietly.

Is this a diet?

No. Feel Great is presented as time-based eating, not calorie counting. You change when you eat, with three drinks supporting the window, rather than following a list of forbidden foods.

How much caffeine is in Unimate?

Regular Unimate is presented as about a cup of premium coffee per serving. No milligram figure is given in Unicity's profiles for regular Unimate, except for Decaf: under 15 mg per serving. If you are caffeine-sensitive, use Decaf, especially in the evening, and talk to your provider if you are unsure.

How do I mix Unimate and Balance?

Balance: powder into water, mix and drink right away, with a shaker or frother, because it gels in the gut, not in the cup. For exact volumes, follow the pack label — Unicity's own documents give different Unimate volumes in different places, so this site does not publish a single volume as settled.

Will fiber cause bloating?

New fiber can cause temporary bloating, gas, loose stools, or constipation. Unicity's clinician guidance is to start gradually, stay hydrated, and expect symptoms to ease as the gut adjusts. If symptoms persist or worry you, talk to your provider.

Can I use this with medication, or if I am pregnant or nursing?

Talk to your provider first. Unicity presents these products for healthy adults, and states it does not test products on children, pregnant women, or nursing women, so it cannot recommend use for those groups. For medication timing around Balance, Unicity's own materials disagree on the exact hour gap, so ask your provider — do not use a number from this site.

What if I miss a day or a dose?

As presented in Unicity's materials: missing the fast length on one day does not lose everything — keep going, and avoid missing two days in a row. Missing a dose: just resume at the next scheduled use.

How do I track progress?

Unicity's 31-day trackers tick first Unimate, second Unimate, Balance, first meal, last meal, and fast time each day, with day 1 vs day 31 measurements of weight, blood pressure, HbA1c (a 3-month average blood sugar test), and waist. There is no food diary or calorie target in those tools. A Feel Great Fasting app is also offered.

What does it cost?

This site does not quote prices, because Unicity's library set contains no general retail price list, and prices change. Current prices, one-time and subscription, are on Unicity's product page at checkout. Ordering through a distributor link like Mhel's unlocks Member pricing, which just means cheaper than retail — you don't pay extra for the code. That is also why shop links go straight to Unicity.

Is there a guarantee?

This site does not promise a specific guarantee, because Unicity's materials name a 90-day guarantee in some places without stating its terms in the sampled text, and a separate 60-day satisfaction form exists with its own conditions. Check the terms on Unicity's site at checkout before you buy on a guarantee.

Does this replace my medicine or my doctor?

No. This is a supplement, not a drug, and not a substitute for medical care. Do not stop or reduce medication on the basis of a product page. Unicity's clinician materials say providers should tailor recommendations and monitor markers, and that any medication adjustment is a clinician decision.
