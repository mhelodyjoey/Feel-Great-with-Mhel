What to expect over 90 days

No promises. Just a gentle rhythm, with support, in order, so you know what the first 90 days are actually for. Your numbers are yours — celebrate them, don't copy mine.

Days 1–14: Get the rhythm

Morning Unimate, lunch Unimate, Balance with your biggest meal, window closed after dinner. That's all you need to do at first. New fiber can cause temporary bloating or gas — start gradually, drink water, and expect that to ease as your gut adjusts, as Unicity's clinician guidance puts it. If it worries you, talk to your provider.

Days 15–30: Track gently, with love

By now the routine should feel less like a chore. This is where the tracker becomes your best friend: tick each day, write your day 30 numbers, and compare them to day 1 — weight, waist, blood pressure, and a 3-month average blood sugar test if your provider orders one. No food diary, no calorie target in Unicity's tracker tools.

Days 31–60: Consistency, not perfection

Miss one day? Keep going. Unicity's materials put it plainly: missing one day does not lose everything, avoid missing two in a row. Miss a dose? Resume at the next scheduled use. This stretch is about keeping the window, not about being perfect.

Days 61–90: Your progress, in your own words

Day 90 is your second full check-in after day 30 and day 60. Write your numbers, write how you feel in your own words, and take that to your provider if you have questions about medication or health conditions. Any medication change is a clinician decision, never a product-page decision.

What this is NOT

Not a diet list. Not a drug. Not an injection. Not a substitute for medical care. Not a promise that your numbers will move like mine did — 125 to 83 was my starting point and my result, yours starts with your own baseline.
